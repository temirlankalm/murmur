Murmur — hold a key, talk, get clean text in any app.

INSTALLING

1. Drag Murmur into the Applications folder.

2. macOS will refuse to open it the first time: "Murmur cannot be opened
   because the developer cannot be verified", or "Murmur is damaged".
   Neither is true — the app just isn't notarised by Apple, which needs a
   paid Developer Program membership.

   To clear it, open Terminal and run:

       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Murmur.app

   Then open Murmur normally. You only have to do this once.

3. Murmur asks for two permissions, and needs both:

     Microphone     — to hear you
     Accessibility  — to watch for the trigger key and type into other apps

   Accessibility has to be granted by hand in System Settings →
   Privacy & Security → Accessibility. If Murmur isn't in the list, drag
   it in from Applications.

USING IT

Hold right Option, speak, let go. The text is typed at your cursor.
Press Esc while speaking to throw it away.

There's no window and no Dock icon — Murmur lives in the menu bar. If you
can't see its icon (a full menu bar on a notched MacBook hides it), open
the settings window from Terminal:

    open murmur://settings

REQUIREMENTS

macOS 26 or later, Apple Silicon.

Source, and an explanation of every setting:
https://github.com/temirlankalm/murmur
